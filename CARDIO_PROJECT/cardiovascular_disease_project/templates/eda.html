Understanding Cardiovascular Disease (CVD)

Cardiovascular disease encompasses disorders of the heart and vascular system. Key clinical drivers include persistent high blood pressure, elevated cholesterol levels, elevated fasting glucose, physical inactivity, and obesity.

Key Findings from Dataset Analysis:
  • Systolic blood pressure (ap_hi) is the single strongest predictor of CVD risk (68.9% importance).
  • Patient age is the second most significant predictor (12.8% contribution).
  • Body Mass Index (BMI) engineered from height and weight significantly improves model precision.
